Chipi uses the English 18M Pocket TTS model and Alba voice by Kyutai,
converted to Core ML by FluidInference. Chipi distributes the selected model
files and voice without changing their weights.

Pocket TTS by Kyutai: https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts
Core ML conversion: https://huggingface.co/FluidInference/pocket-tts-coreml/tree/c2e998e269e4d4b51f74061faddb8450cd8f9a9a
