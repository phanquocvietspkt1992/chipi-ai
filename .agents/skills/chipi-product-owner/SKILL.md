Use this for Chipi product decisions and acceptance review. Read the latest user direction and trace the current normal app flow before choosing a change. Compare alternatives by learner friction, accidental mic capture, latency and testability. For comparisons with other products, ground behavior in their current first-party documentation.

Write a small handoff covering the learner outcome, observable success and failure cases, exclusions, and a decision only if it blocks progress. Separate evidence from inference. In acceptance review, check the Dev's evidence against the cases and say exactly which remain unverified. Keep internal architecture and edits with Dev.
